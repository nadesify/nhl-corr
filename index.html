NHL Stack Correlations

DraftKings classic scoring, real game logs
How to read this
  • Pick a team. Rows and columns are its players, grouped by tonight's lines when we have them.
  • Adjusted corr (default): how much two players' DraftKings points rise and fall together. Red = they tend to score together, blue = when one scores the other usually doesn't. A pair with only a few games together sits near the normal number for that kind of pair (linemates, power play, D pair), and moves away from it only as real games pile up.
  • Pts boost: the column player's average points when the row player scores 10+ (slide it), minus when he doesn't. Faded cells = thin sample: a hint, not a rule.
  • Tap a name to rank who rises with that player; tap a cell for every game behind the number.
  • "Opp G" is whoever started in goal against this team that night.
  • Lines change and players get traded: use Last 20 games to see the current group, and always check how many games a number is built on. Correlation helps you pick stacks; it doesn't tell you who will score tonight, and it is a reading aid, not a proven edge.
